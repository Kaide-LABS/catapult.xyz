# GDPR Compliance Statement

## 5.1 DPO Appointment
Acme AI has appointed a Data Protection Officer. They can be contacted at compliance@acme-ai.com.

## 5.2 Data Subject Rights
We provide an automated portal for data access, deletion, and portability requests, guaranteeing a 30-day SLA for fulfillment.

## 5.3 Legal Basis
Our legal basis relies on legitimate interest for product analytics and explicit consent for marketing communications.

## 5.4 Cross-border Transfers
Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for non-adequate countries.

## 5.5 Sub-processors
Sub-processors are listed in Appendix A. We enforce a 30-day notification period for any sub-processor changes.

## 5.6 DPIA
A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is completed for our core AI processing pipeline and reviewed annually.
